This is a reporting detail draft written during collection, after some individual results were observed. It is not a new preregistration, an approved statistical result, or a change to any trial/condition/order/gold/gate. The original measurement-preregistration SHA remains90d4c91bf8b0c9495e0cb95376a55f84d17351308541414d58dd650d8e2dd37c. No numerical interval is computed here. Independent read-only review is required before implementation.

Report raw status and evidence validation separately; never rewrite an original record, replace an ID, turn a timeout into observed completion or silently omit invalid evidence. If fullraw validation rejects a nominal raw success, retain both facts and the reason, preserve its denominator, and defer classification to actual evidence review.
